1. The service

Clepsydre is a free escape-room style game: you go through timed mini-game rooms (riddles, mental maths, 2048, checkmate, maze, pinball, padlocks, sliding puzzle…) before the water clock runs dry, in a solo game or an online game with other players. The game offers leaderboards, titles, avatars, a daily wheel, friends and key gifts. It is funded by advertising and optional in-app purchases. Detailed rules for games, scores and rewards are in the game rules.

2. Account

  • Purchases in euros require an account signed in with Google or Apple.
  • An account is personal. Using several accounts to obtain advantages (referrals, rewards, key gifts) is forbidden.
  • You can delete your account at any time (Settings › Delete my account) or by email: see the dedicated page. Deletion is permanent and the credits, keys, items and purchases linked to the account are lost, without refund.

Your nickname and avatar are visible to other players, especially in leaderboards. Nicknames must not be offensive, hateful, discriminatory, sexual, promotional, or impersonate someone else. We may change a nickname, remove scores or suspend an account that breaks these rules.

4. Virtual currency, keys and items

Credits, keys, titles, avatars and frames are virtual items usable only in Clepsydre. They have no monetary value and cannot be exchanged (except giving a key to a friend as provided by the game), refunded or converted into money. Leaderboard rewards are only virtual credits and titles: Clepsydre is not a gambling game.

5. Virtual players

So that everyone can play at any time, virtual players may fill a session or appear in leaderboards when there are not enough participants. They receive no rewards (see the game rules).
