Clepsydre privacy policy

Last updated: November 28, 2026

This policy explains what data Clepsydre collects, why, who it is shared with and how to exercise your rights. It applies to the Android and iPhone apps (com.monentcie.clepsydre). The French version is the reference version.

1. Who are we?

Clepsydre is published by Monent (Yann Gousset, sole trader – French micro-entreprise, 10 rue Claire Lacombe, 51000 Châlons-en-Champagne, France, SIRET 103 094 090 00018), the data controller.
Contact: contact@monentcie.com

2. Data we collect

DataWhenWhy
Firebase account ID (anonymous account by default)On first launchTo identify you and save your progress without sign-up. The account is managed by Google Firebase Authentication.
Google or Apple ID and the associated email addressIf you sign in with Google or AppleTo keep your account across devices and allow purchases in euros. We never see your password.
Game profile: nickname, avatar, frame, titles, credits, keys, purchased itemsWhen you play and customise your profileTo run the game, the shop and your profile.
Scores per room and online game historyAt the end of each room of an online gameTo compute the session, daily, monthly, yearly and all-time leaderboards and award rewards.
Friends list, friend code, key giftsWhen you add a friend or give a keyTo run friends and gifts.
Referral: the account that invited you, number of referralsWhen you install the game from an invitation link, or when a referred friend finishes their first gameTo award the referral reward, once per referred friend.
Purchases: store order or transaction ID, product, dateFor each purchase in eurosTo credit and secure the purchase and handle refunds. Monent never sees any banking data.
Advertising ID and technical device dataCollected by Google AdMob, according to your consentTo show ads and measure their performance (section 4).

Solo game scores are stored on your device only: they are not sent to us and do not appear in the global leaderboard.

Clepsydre collects no location data and does not access your contacts, photos or microphone.

3. Who sees what?

  • The global leaderboard (day, month, year, all time) is visible to all players: it shows the nickname, avatar, title and score.
  • The leaderboard of an online session is visible only to the players of that session.
  • Your friends see your nickname and avatar. Your email address is never shown to other players.

4. Advertising

Clepsydre is partly funded by advertising: banners and optional rewarded videos provided by Google AdMob. Google may use your device's advertising ID and technical data to show ads, personalised or not depending on your choice, and to prevent fraud.

  • In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, a Google consent form (UMP) asks for your choice. You can review it at any time in Settings › Ad choices.
  • On iPhone, Apple first asks whether you allow tracking (App Tracking Transparency). If you refuse, only non-personalised ads are shown and the Google form is not displayed.
  • The “No ads” purchase removes banners; rewarded videos remain optional.

You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone settings. Learn more: how Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

5. Purchases

Purchases in euros are processed by Google Play (Google Play Billing) or the App Store (Apple In-App Purchase). We only receive the order ID, the product and the date; your banking details stay with Google or Apple. The terms of sale are set out in the terms of sale.

6. Service providers and sharing

We do not sell your data. It is processed by the following services, acting on our behalf or as partners:

  • Google Firebase: Authentication (accounts), Cloud Firestore (profiles, scores, leaderboards) and Cloud Functions (score, reward and purchase processing), hosted in the europe-west1 (Belgium) region — Firebase privacy;
  • Google AdMob (ads) — Google privacy policy;
  • Google Play Billing and Apple In-App Purchase (payments);
  • Sign in with Google and Sign in with Apple (sign-in) — Apple privacy policy.

Some processing by Google or Apple may take place outside the European Union; it is covered by the European Commission's standard contractual clauses or the EU–US Data Privacy Framework.

7. Legal bases

  • Performance of the service: account, profile, games, leaderboards, friends, referrals, purchases.
  • Legal obligation: keeping the purchase register (accounting).
  • Legitimate interest: security, anti-cheating, non-personalised ads that fund the free game.
  • Consent: personalised ads (which you can withdraw at any time).

8. Retention period

Your data is kept as long as your account exists. When you delete your account, it is erased; scores already published in leaderboards are anonymised (the nickname is replaced with “—”). The purchase register (order ID, product, date) is kept for as long as required by our accounting obligations and for handling refunds.

9. Delete your account and data

You can delete your account directly in the app (Settings › Delete my account) or ask us by email, without the app. The steps are described on the dedicated page:

🗑️ Delete my account

10. Your rights

Under the GDPR, you can request access to, rectification, erasure or portability of your data, object to or restrict processing, and withdraw your consent, by writing to contact@monentcie.com. You can also lodge a complaint with the French data protection authority, the CNIL, or your local authority.

11. Age

Clepsydre is not intended for children under 13. In France, minors under 15 need their parents' consent to use the app, and any purchase by a minor must be authorised by their parents. If you believe a child has sent us data, contact us so that we can delete it.

12. Security

Exchanges between the app and our servers are encrypted (HTTPS). Scores, rewards and purchases are checked server-side, and security rules restrict data access: each player can only read and change what concerns them.

13. Changes

We may change this policy. The date at the top of the page will then be updated; for any significant change, we will inform you in the app.
