Clepsydre

The timed escape game: 21 mini-game rooms, solo or online, before the water clock runs dry.

Play solo through 20 random rooms in 1 h 30, or online with up to 15 players: riddles, mental maths, 2048, checkmate, maze, pinball, padlocks, sliding puzzle… Daily, monthly, yearly and all-time leaderboards, titles to unlock, avatars, a daily wheel and friends.
