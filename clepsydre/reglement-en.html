Article 1 – Organiser

The Clepsydre game (Android and iPhone, com.monentcie.clepsydre) is published and run by Monent (Yann Gousset, sole trader – French micro-entreprise, 10 rue Claire Lacombe, 51000 Châlons-en-Champagne, France, SIRET 103 094 090 00018) (the “Publisher”), contact: contact@monentcie.com. Google and Apple are not sponsors and are not involved in any way in running the leaderboards and rewards. This is a translation; the French version prevails.

Article 2 – Participation

The game is free. Just install the app; an account is created automatically. The rewards described below are only virtual credits and titles, with no monetary value: no real prize or money is at stake. These rules supplement the terms of use.

Article 3 – How the game works

Clepsydre is a timed escape game: players go through mini-game rooms before the water clock (the game time) runs dry. The game has 21 rooms: riddles, mental maths, slingshot, 2048, checkmate, fight, drag race, piano keys, general knowledge, climb, flying gates, hungry maze, pinball, dungeon defence, colour memory, western saloon, padlock, sliding puzzle, clock tower, snake and anagrams.

Article 4 – Solo game

  • A solo game has 20 randomly drawn rooms, to be played within 1 h 30.
  • It is free and requires no key.
  • Solo scores are stored on the device and do not count towards the global leaderboard.

Article 5 – Online game

  • Entering an online game costs 1 key.
  • Three formats: 30 minutes / 10 rooms, 1 hour / 15 rooms, 1 h 30 / 20 rooms.
  • A session holds up to 15 players. A room occupied by a player is unavailable to the others while they play it.
  • The session leaderboard is visible only to its players.

Article 6 – Rooms and scores

Each room lasts 3 minutes at most and earns 0 to 10,000 points depending on the player's performance. A game's score is the sum of its room scores. Scores are checked by the server.

Article 7 – Leaderboards and rewards

Only online games count towards the daily, monthly, yearly and all-time leaderboards. Periods end according to Paris time. At the end of each period, the top three receive:

Yearly300,000 credits200,000 credits100,000 credits“Top N of the year”
All timeNo rewardNo title

“N” is the rank achieved (1, 2 or 3).

Article 8 – Virtual players

🤖

So that everyone can play at any time, virtual players may fill a session or appear in leaderboards when there are not enough participants. They receive no rewards: leaderboard credits and titles are awarded only to real players, in the order of their ranking.

Article 9 – Earning credits

Every player starts with 0 credits. Credits are earned as follows:

ActionReward
Finish a game+10 credits
Score 10,000 points in a room+20 credits and the room's title
Daily wheel0, 50 or 100 credits
Watch a rewarded ad (optional)+25 credits, up to 10 times a day
Referral+100 credits per referred friend, once they finish their first game (once per friend, no limit on the number of friends)
LeaderboardsSee Article 7

Credits can be used to buy keys (100 credits per key), avatars and frames. They can also be purchased: see the terms of sale. They have no monetary value and cannot be converted into money.

Article 10 – Fair play

Any cheating or attempt to cheat (modifying the app, automated tools, exploiting bugs, multiple accounts, referring your own accounts…) leads to the removal of the scores and rewards concerned, or even deletion of the account, without refund.

Article 11 – Changes to the game

The Publisher may change these rules, the rooms, game formats, earnings and rewards, or suspend the game. Any change takes effect when published; rewards already awarded are kept.

Article 12 – Governing law

These rules are governed by French law. For any question or complaint: contact@monentcie.com.
