Delete your Clepsydre account

App: Clepsydre (Android and iPhone com.monentcie.clepsydre) · Publisher: Monent

You can delete your Clepsydre account and the data linked to it, at any time and free of charge, from the app or without installing it.

From the app (immediate)

  • 1

    Open Clepsydre and go to Settings.

  • 2

    Tap “Delete my account” and confirm. Deletion is immediate and permanent.

By email (without the app)

Send an email to contact@monentcie.com with the subject “Clepsydre account deletion”, stating your nickname and your friend code (shown on the app's Friends screen, “My friend code”), so that we can find your account. If your account is linked to Google or Apple, please write from that account's email address if possible.

✉️ Write the request

⏱️

We process email requests within 30 days at most and confirm the deletion by email.

Data deleted

  • your account: Firebase ID and, if linked, Google or Apple ID and email address;
  • your game profile: nickname, avatar, frame, titles, credits, keys and purchased items;
  • your room scores and online game history;
  • your friends list, friend code and referral information.

Data kept

  • Scores already published in leaderboards remain displayed but are anonymised: your nickname is replaced with “—”.
  • The purchase register (order or transaction ID, product, date) is kept for as long as required by our accounting obligations and for handling refunds, without your nickname.

Credits, keys, items and “No ads” linked to the account are lost and not refunded. Solo game scores, stored only on your phone, are erased by uninstalling the app. Advertising data is managed by Google: you can reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone settings.

Delete only some data

You can ask us by email, at the same address, to erase only part of your data (for example your email address or nickname) without deleting your account.

To learn more about the data collected, see the privacy policy.
